TMDB LOGO - NOTICE
==================

tmdb-logo.svg is TMDB's approved "alt short" logo, as TMDB serves it on
https://www.themoviedb.org/about/logos-attribution and as Silo's web client ships it
(web/src/assets/tmdb/tmdb-logo.svg of https://github.com/Silo-Server/silo-server).

It is the same drawing, with one change of form, not of look: the gradient is set on its shape as an
attribute instead of through a <style> sheet, which Windows' SVG renderer (Direct2D) does not read and
would otherwise draw the logo in black.

covers Granary's source code. Granary shows it only beside a TMDB score, where the score came from,
as TMDB's terms allow, and keeps it smaller than Granary's own branding. Keep its look unmodified:
its colours and proportions.

Granary uses TMDB's data through your Silo server; it is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
